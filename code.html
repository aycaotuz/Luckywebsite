Our Journey

A Twelve-Year Odyssey

Lucky entered our lives as a bundle of white and brown energy exactly twelve years ago. From the very first day, he chose 'Dad' as his primary human, forming a bond that has become the bedrock of our home. Whether it was navigating the bustling streets of new cities or quiet afternoons in the garden, they have been inseparable.

As a Jack Russell, his spirit remains undiminished by age. He carries the wisdom of a senior but the heart of a pup, always ready to protect the family or find the perfect sunspot for a nap. His journey isn't just about time; it's about the thousands of small moments that make a life whole.
